01 — INFORMATION

Information we process

Account and authentication information

Depending on the sign-in method you choose, Google Firebase Authentication processes your email address and password credentials, phone number and SMS verification information, or Google Account identifier and associated account information. We do not receive your plain-text password.

Profile information

We collect the profile details you submit: player name, age, sex, nationality and nationality flag, plus an optional profile photo. The local path to a photo stays on the device, while a copy of the selected profile photo may be encoded and stored with your cloud profile for cross-device sync and opponent display.

Fitness and gameplay information

We process squat, push-up and jumping-jack counts; total repetitions; highest daily squat count; matches played; login streak; achievements; ranking points and rank; match results, dates, scores and opponent details; and whether you accepted the safety disclaimer. This is fitness and app-activity information used to provide progress tracking and competition features.

Online matchmaking information

To create and operate online matches, we process your Firebase user ID, selected workout, queue and session identifiers, name, nationality, flag, ranking score, live repetition score, connection status, forfeit status, and join, finish or leave timestamps.

Camera and pose information

The App requests front-camera access to detect body joints and count exercise repetitions. Camera frames and generated pose landmarks are processed locally using Google ML Kit pose detection. Work Out Arena does not upload, store or share the camera feed or pose landmarks. The App does not request or use microphone access for pose detection.

Settings and local information

Language, music and sound-effect volume, onboarding state and other preferences are stored locally on your device. Google and Firebase services may also process limited technical information such as IP address, device and app identifiers, network information, and security or diagnostic events as needed to authenticate users, prevent abuse and operate their services.

02 — PURPOSES

How we use information

  • Authenticate your account and provide password reset or phone verification.
  • Create, display and synchronize your profile across devices.
  • Count repetitions, provide form guidance and maintain fitness progress.
  • Find opponents, run live matches, detect disconnections and determine results.
  • Calculate rankings, achievements and match history.
  • Maintain service security, prevent duplicate or abusive matchmaking and troubleshoot failures.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to valid user requests.

03 — DISCLOSURE

When information is visible or disclosed

Other players

Your chosen player name, profile photo, nationality and flag, ranking score and rank may be shown to other users during matchmaking and matches. Your current repetition score, connection status and match outcome are also shared with the matched opponent. Do not place sensitive information in your player name or profile photo.

Service providers

We use Google services including Firebase Authentication, Firebase Realtime Database, Google Sign-In and Google ML Kit. Google processes information on our behalf or as an independent provider under its applicable terms and privacy documentation. Review Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a merger, acquisition or transfer of the App, subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not sell personal information or share it with advertising networks for targeted advertising.

04 — STORAGE

Storage, security and international processing

Cloud account and gameplay information is stored using Firebase. The App’s Realtime Database is configured in the Asia Southeast region, although Google may process supporting authentication, security and service information in other locations. Your information may therefore be processed outside your country or region.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the service, including encrypted network connections, Firebase authentication and database access controls. Local information is protected by your device’s security. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

05 — RETENTION

How long we retain information

Account, profile, fitness statistics, ranking and match-history information is generally retained while your account remains active and until it is deleted. Matchmaking queue entries are intended to be short-lived; account-linked match sessions and operational records may remain until routine cleanup or account deletion. Camera frames and pose landmarks are discarded after on-device processing and are not retained by us. Local preferences remain until you clear the App’s storage or uninstall it.

Following a verified deletion request, we aim to delete the account and associated account-linked data within 30 days. We may retain limited information longer only where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal compliance, and will delete or anonymize it when that need ends. Deleting the App alone does not delete cloud account data.

06 — YOUR CONTROL

Your choices and rights

  • You can edit profile information in the App.
  • You can decline camera permission, but camera-based repetition counting will not work.
  • You can omit a profile photo and choose “Prefer not to say” or “Not specified” where offered.
  • You can stop online disclosure by not entering online matchmaking.
  • You may request access, correction or deletion by contacting us.

The App provides fitness tracking and entertainment features. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Please follow the in-app safety guidance and seek professional advice where appropriate.
