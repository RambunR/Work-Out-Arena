Delete your account

You can request permanent deletion of your Work Out Arena account and its associated cloud data.

HOW TO REQUEST DELETION

Send us a verified request

  1. Email us from the address connected to your account where possible. Use the button below or email contactus@hknovaxrproduction.com with the subject “Work Out Arena Account Deletion Request”.
  2. Identify the account. Include your player name and the email address or phone number used to sign in. Do not send your password or SMS verification code.
  3. Complete verification if requested. We may ask for limited additional information to confirm that you own the account and to protect accounts from unauthorized deletion.
  4. Deletion is completed within 30 days after verification. We will confirm when the request is complete. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
Email deletion request

WHAT WILL BE DELETED

Account-linked data

  • Your Firebase Authentication account and account identifiers.
  • Your player profile, including name, age, sex, nationality, flag and uploaded profile photo.
  • Your workout totals, achievements, login streak, ranking score and safety acceptance record.
  • Your stored match history and account-linked matchmaking, score, presence and session data.
  • Your public opponent profile used for online matchmaking.

Information may be retained only where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal compliance. Any retained information will be limited, protected and deleted or anonymized when the retention need ends.

DEVICE DATA

Clear local information

A cloud deletion request does not automatically remove copies stored on a device that is offline. After receiving confirmation, clear Work Out Arena’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the App from each device. Camera frames and pose landmarks are processed temporarily on-device and are not stored in your cloud account.
